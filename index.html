  10. Step 4 · Cables

    Hooking it up

    Record player cable

    It comes attached to the record player. Keep the record player within about 3 feet of the amp.

    Nothing to buy

    Speaker wire

    Runs from the amp to each speaker. One 50 foot spool does both. Get 16 gauge, made of oxygen-free copper.

    Find on Amazon

    Setup: plug the record player into the input marked PHONO. Then set the EQ switch on the record player to OFF.

    Speaker wire has one side marked with a stripe or text. Put that side on red (+) at both ends.

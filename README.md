# Turntable

A simple shopping list I made to help my brother buy his wife a record player. It points him to gear that sounds good and will last, in the order to buy it.

It's a single static page, hosted with GitHub Pages.

## Notes

- Prices and stock were checked in October 2026 and will go out of date.
- Links are plain store links. No affiliate links, no tracking.
- Product photos and names belong to their makers.
- This is a personal page and will come down once the shopping is done.
